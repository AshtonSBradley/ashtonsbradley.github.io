PhD student Nils Krause has developed a reservoir theory of a highly nonlinear reservoir interaction process [Evaporative damping in open systems theory of Bose-Einstein condensates](https://arxiv.org/abs/2512.06220). 

Previously regarded as unimportant (a weake surface term), evaporative damping is shown to be comparable to number damping in regimes of high phase space density. Both processes are weaker than energy damping, which is the dominant reservoir interaction in this regime.

Near the critical point each of the three reservoir interaction process is important.

This completes the first-principles reservoir c-field theory of Bose-Einstein condensates. Congratulations Nils!
