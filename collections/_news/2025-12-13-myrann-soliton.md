I travelled to Paris for Myrann's PhD thesis defence on experiments in quantum fluids of light. Myrann gave a superb talk. Then came the celebration of the ground-breaking research Myrann has pioneered in the group of Quentin Glorieux at Ecole Normale Supérieure - Sorbonne Université. 


This was also an opportunity to attend the conference

[Soliton - Maths-physics meeting on solitons in condensates and polaritons](https://solitons.sciencesconf.org/) 

at Institut Henri Poincaré. The meeting involved an equal mixture of quantum physicists and mathematicians, a full day of talks, and a lot of coffee.
