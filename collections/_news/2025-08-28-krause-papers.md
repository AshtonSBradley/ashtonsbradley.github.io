In the last week Nils had two papers accepted for publication:

- [Equilibrium, Relaxation and Fluctuations in homogeneous Bose-Einstein Condensates: Linearized Classical Field Analysis, Nils A. Krause, Ashton S Bradley](http://arxiv.org/abs/2505.03216).
- [Velocity correlations of vortices and rarefaction pulses in compressible planar quantum fluids, Ashton S Bradley, Nils A. Krause](http://arxiv.org/abs/2502.08930)

The first article develops a c-field treatment of a homogeneous Bose gas with careful attention to the energy cutoff and the role of different dissipation mechanisms in equilibration. Consistent with our other recent work, energy damping is found to be the dominant reservoir interaction at high phase space density. 

The second article develops an analytical treatment of kinetic energy spectra and velocity correlations for both vortices and rarefaction pulses in planer BECs. Our results agree well with the experimental observations from the [Glorieux group at LKB](https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysRevLett.134.233401).

Congratulations Nils!
