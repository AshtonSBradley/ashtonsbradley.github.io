This month we had two papers appear in Physical Review Letters!

- [Yu et al, Emergent Non-Eulerian Hydrodynamics of Quantum Vortices in Two Dimensions](https://link.aps.org/doi/10.1103/PhysRevLett.119.185301) where we generalise the anomalous hydrodynamics theory of Wiegman and Abanov to neutral systems of vortices. The intermediate scale vortex fluid hydrodynamic exhibits anomalous stresses due to the non-analyticity of quantum vortex cores.

- [Reeves et al, Enstrophy Cascade in Decaying Two-Dimensional Quantum Turbulence](https://link.aps.org/doi/10.1103/PhysRevLett.119.184502)
where we carry out large-scale point-vortex simulations and observe a direct enstrophy cascade in decaying two-dimensional quantum turbulence.

Congratulations to Xiaoquan, Matt and the rest of the team!
