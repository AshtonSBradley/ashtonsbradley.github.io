Ashton has been awarded a Visiting Professor Fellowship at the Quantum Information Centre Sorbonne (QICS) for 2026. The fellowship supports travel and accommodation costs for visits of up to three months to a Sorbonne University Alliance laboratory. 

Ashton will visit Quentin Glorieux's research group to develop theory of superfluid excitations in quantum fluids of light. 

More information about the scheme can be found here:

- [Visiting Professors Programme](https://qics.sorbonne-universite.fr/en/research/visiting-professors-program)
