Ashton has been awarded a Visiting Professor Fellowship at the Quantum Information Centre Sorbonne (QICS) for 2026. The fellowship funds visits of up to three months to a Sorbonne University Alliance laboratory. Ashton will visit Quentin Glorieux's research group to research quantum fluids of light. 

More information about the scheme can be found here:

- [Visiting Professors Programme](https://qics.sorbonne-universite.fr/en/research/visiting-professors-program)
