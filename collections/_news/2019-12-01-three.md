In 2019 several experimental collaborations came to fruition all at once, and we published two articles in Science, and one in Nature Communications in the same year:

- [Giant vortex clusters in a two-dimensional quantum fluid, Guillaume Gauthier, Matthew T. Reeves, Xiaoquan Yu, A. S. Bradley, Mark Baker, Thomas A. Bell, Halina Rubinsztein‐Dunlop, Matthew J. Davis, Tyler W. Neely
Science 364, 6447 1264 (2019)](https://doi.org/10.1126/science.aat5718)

- [Coherent vortex dynamics in a strongly interacting superfluid on a silicon chip, Yauhen P. Sachkou, Christopher G. Baker, Glen I. Harris, Oliver R. Stockdale, Stefan Forstner, Matthew T. Reeves, Xin He, David L. McAuslan, Ashton S. Bradley, Matthew J. Davis, Warwick P. Bowen,
Science 366, 1480 (2019)](https://doi.org/10.1126/science.aaw9229)

- [Thermally robust spin correlations between two 85 Rb atoms in an optical microtrap, Pimopan Sompet, Stuart S. Szigeti, Eyal Schwartz, Ashton S. Bradley, and Mikkel F. Andersen,  Nat Commun. 10, 1889 (2019)](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41467-019-09420-6)
