Plan basis & assumptions

Direct sunlight comes from approximately north-east, following the supplied orientation. The model uses an illustrative 25° sun elevation, with shadows towards south-west; this is not a date-specific solar study. Electric and ambient lighting are unchanged.

The supplied September 12 screenshot establishes room placement, outer boundaries and the adjoining hallway. The room interiors use the developed concepts; the screenshot’s benches and other fittings are not copied.

1.21: 8.363 × 11.998 m after moving the north boundary and door layout 500 mm south to enlarge the store. Six main desk rows at 1.20 m pitch replace seven rows at 1.00 m pitch; the extra 200 mm per row interval is added behind the unchanged 450 mm-deep desks. Each raised tier starts at the front edge of its desk, with the first row on the level floor. The rear platform now ends at its desk front, 2.30 m from the south wall, retaining its +600 mm height and six 100 mm rises. Rear-row chairs match the other teal chairs. Its ten seats now also have a desk. Total capacity is 68 desk seats plus two fold-down positions (70 places); the fold-downs occupy the wheelchair bay when deployed. Fully seated instead shows 68 desk occupants and one wheelchair user (69 people), with both fold-downs stowed. Fully seated + overflow adds ten white chairs with 700 mm-high seats behind the rear desk row (79 people). Overflow chairs are spaced at 780 mm centres, extending into both rear side aisles. They have no desks and restrict rear circulation; this is an illustrative layout, not an approved capacity. Backup stacks are hidden when overflow is deployed. Paired 3.10 m teaching screens are retained at the relocated north wall; the rear projector is replaced by a permanent whiteboard. Row gaps remain constrained and require occupied-access review.

1.22: approximately 8.40 × 15.00 m, with the existing experiment bays and ancillary store. 1.23: 10.75 × 9.00 m with 24 teaching places. The green bench strips are computer and service partitions for power and network; no PC displays are included. A single 560 × 500 mm column spans the 1.21/1.22 wall south of the confirmed 1.40 m emergency doorway. The shared-wall bay immediately beside the connecting door is a permanent whiteboard on both faces up to the 2.20 m door head in every option. In the clear-glass-and-curtain option, glazed bays extend from floor to the 3.00 m ceiling, with a full-height curtain and no solid plinth. In the smart-glass option, only the next two bays (2.80 m nominal width) switch, between the 1.00 m handle height and the 2.20 m door head; surrounding glazing stays clear.

The store north of 1.21 has a single 6.10 m-long, 1.20 m-deep open rack along the north wall, extending to the east wall without an end gap, leaving approximately 1.63 m clear aisle before the parked equipment. The store is now approximately 3.00 m north–south (500 mm wider), with the rack’s door-end position unchanged. The store entrance is widened from 900 to 1,400 mm, extending northward from its retained south hinge and keeping its inward swing. The lift clearance test uses the same widened opening. The printer station in 1.22 moves to the south side of the fixed column, replacing the low green storage and clearing the relocated fire exit. A compact hand-operated fork lift is shown at the east end (assumed footprint 1.35 × 0.65 m). Equipment dimensions and manoeuvring space are illustrative.

The ceiling is 3.00 m above the level floor, leaving 2.40 m above the +600 mm rear platform. The upper hallway is approximately 1.7 m wide, estimated from the screenshot. Alignments, wall thicknesses and glazing heights remain approximate; lighting and materials are illustrative.
