Dimensions & assumptions

Ceiling height is 3.0 m. The coordinated design includes 36 benches / 144 illustrative places, revised G.04 tables, demonstrator and displays. G.04 south-wall low storage is removed. The added foyer follows the 17 September plan; its cropped western edge remains open and undefined. Toilet interiors are context only. G.01 is labelled female and G.02 male, as corrected. The drinking fountain sits at the former G.01a text position.

The west stairs rise east toward the lift lobby to an intermediate landing half a floor above the foyer, aligned with the display-cabinet wall. You can walk up and down the flight. The model uses a 1.5 m rise with 10 risers.

Both options retain the existing entrance. The current design has a full-width pair of glass doors beside G.02 into G.C, opening outward west into the main foyer. The 3.127 m model opening has two equal leaves of approximately 1.563 m; the entrance operator is to be confirmed. Both current lift-lobby doors are matching single glass leaves opening inward into the lobby. In the proposed option, push-button mechanical operators open the hinged leaves; they do not slide.

Gold lobby push pads are provided on both sides, centred 1.0 m above the floor and offset from the door openings. Use the model controls to preview the outward-opening entrance doors. Pressing a lobby pad opens and holds that door; use “Close doors” to reset. The guided tour operates the doors for the route. These are access design intentions, not verified accessibility, fire-egress or structural compliance.

The solid south-hallway door with its narrow vision panel is retained. The model uses approximately 1 × 2 m benches and 1.65 m eye height. Furniture heights, finishes and lighting are illustrative. This is a registered concept model, not a surveyed dimensional model.
