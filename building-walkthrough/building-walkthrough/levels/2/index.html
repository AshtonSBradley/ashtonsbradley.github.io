Dimensions & assumptions

Direct sunlight comes from approximately north-east, following the supplied orientation. The model uses an illustrative 25° sun elevation, with shadows towards south-west; this is not a date-specific solar study. Electric and ambient lighting are unchanged.

Based on the latest page 2 plan: 30 lecture seats, 10 meeting seats, paired screens with whiteboards, and the fixed column. Walkthrough revision: lectern moved 650 mm north to clear the south aisle; two sliding panels per whiteboard (four panels total), with tracks to the ceiling. Panel sizes and mechanisms are illustrative. The west double-door entry is set 1000 mm west of the original room boundary in a glazed recess. Both 900 mm leaves still open inward, but their swings remain outside the original teaching-room floor area. The doorway width and 30-seat layout are unchanged. This recess occupies west-side circulation space; its fit, approach space and door requirements need checking against the building plan.

Ceiling height: 3.0 m, as specified. Assumed desks: 0.74 m; seated eye level: 1.20 m; standing eye level: 1.65 m. Door heights, screen heights, chair sizes, ceiling finish, lighting and materials are illustrative. Plan positions are transcribed geometry, not a measured survey.

Both north-facing glass walls and their doors have frosted privacy bands from 750 to 1850 mm above the floor, matching the wave-superposition pattern on Level 3. Frosting is denser at eye level, with clear glass above and below. The side entrances remain clear glass and the south high-level glazing is unchanged. This is partial visual screening; film light transmission and optical diffusion are not simulated. Corridor context is simplified. Navigation uses approximate furniture clearances.
